# Code of Conduct

Think Denominator is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free community for everyone.

Contributors should communicate constructively, respect differing
experiences, focus criticism on ideas and code, and protect confidential
information. Abuse, harassment, discrimination, threats, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and
restrict participation when conduct is inconsistent with these
expectations. Concerns can be reported privately to
<rubesh@thinkdenominator.com>. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
handled as confidentially as practical.
