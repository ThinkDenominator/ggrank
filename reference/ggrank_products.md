# Synthetic product rankings

A general-purpose teaching dataset of product sales across three years.
Every product name, ranking, and sales value is synthetic and was
generated specifically for package teaching examples.

## Usage

``` r
ggrank_products
```

## Format

A data frame with 24 rows and 4 variables: `year`, `product`,
`category`, and `sales`.

## Source

Synthetic data created for ggrank.
